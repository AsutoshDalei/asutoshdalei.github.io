# About Asutosh Dalei

Asutosh Dalei is a data scientist and AI engineer based in Washington, DC, United States, with over four years of industry experience in machine learning, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and predictive modeling. He holds a Master's degree in Data Science from the University of Maryland, College Park.

## Experience

Most recently, Asutosh served as a Machine Learning Engineering Intern at Nokia (June 2025 – June 2026), where he built an enterprise-scale "Knowledge Layer" for product documentation that unified vector (OpenSearch), relational (PostgreSQL), object (MinIO), and graph (Neo4j) databases. He benchmarked custom embedding models — Nomic, BGE M3, and Embedding Gemma — improved retrieval relevance with the Qwen3 Reranker, and built a multimodal RAG pipeline using Jina Embeddings V4 and OpenAI CLIP to create a joint vector space for querying tables and figures, deployed as containerized microservices on Google Cloud Platform.

Prior to Nokia, he was a Data Scientist at Maruti Suzuki's R&D Division in Bengaluru, India (January 2022 – August 2024). There he implemented a RAG-based conversational AI assistant that reduced manual searches by 60%, developed an LSTM algorithm for lead-acid battery health prediction deployed across 7,000+ connected cars with 98% system accuracy, and optimized ML training processes to save $48,000 per year.

## Research

His research connects theoretical innovation with practical application. He is the applicant behind patent application 202411040338, "System & Method of Predicting Health Status of Batteries in Vehicles," which applies LSTM deep neural networks to sensor-based telematics data for predictive maintenance. He co-authored "Molecular Signatures and Machine Learning driven Stress Biomarkers for Rainbow Trout Aquaculture and Climate Adaptation" in Nature Scientific Reports (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30120-3) and a survey on federated learning advancements in the Malaysian Journal of Computer Science (https://doi.org/10.22452/mjcs.vol37no1.1).
