# Privacy Statement

This is the personal portfolio website of Asutosh Dalei. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and how you can ask questions about it. Last updated: August 23, 2026.

## What this site collects

- **Analytics:** The home page uses Google Analytics (gtag.js) to measure aggregate traffic such as page views, approximate location, device type, and referring pages. Google may set cookies and process IP addresses for this purpose under its own policies.
- **Contact form:** The contact form on the home page posts through Formspree. If you submit it, your name, email address, and message are forwarded to the site owner's inbox so he can reply. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.
- **No accounts, no ads:** The site has no user accounts, sells nothing, and shows no advertising. It does not sell or share personal data with third parties beyond the services described above.

## Your choices

You can browse most of this site without any data being collected beyond standard server logs kept by GitHub Pages (the hosting provider). You can block Google Analytics with browser settings or extensions such as ad blockers, and decline cookies at the browser level.

## Contacting about privacy

If you have questions about this statement, want your form submission deleted from the inbox, or need anything else related to your data on this site, email asutoshdalei@gmail.com.
